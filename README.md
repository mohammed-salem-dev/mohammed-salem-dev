### What I've been building

**Drop Delivery & Inventory · Marine NanoTech**  
A yacht and villa delivery platform, supported by an inventory system built with **Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL**.

- Centralised product records, SKUs, stock levels, and low-stock monitoring.
- Checkout reservations, payment-triggered stock deductions, and expired-reservation release to prevent overselling and duplicate updates.
- Shopify and Excel imports with validation and duplicate detection, role-based access, and stock-adjustment histories.

**Marine NanoTech website**  
Responsive interfaces, animations, and media-led sections for superyacht surface restoration and protection.
